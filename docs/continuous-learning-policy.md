# Continuous Learning Policy

The learned model recommends. It does not bypass Wade Home Services pricing safeguards, manager review, or rollback controls.

## Validation Workflow

1. A quote is generated by the current rules and any approved model.
2. A manager approves or overrides the recommendation.
3. The customer accepts, declines, or cancels.
4. If accepted, the job is completed.
5. Actual load, labor, disposal, direct cost, final price, and margin are recorded.
6. The record enters a validation queue.
7. An authorized person confirms labels and override reasons.
8. Validated records enter the next candidate snapshot.
9. Retraining occurs only on an approved schedule or after enough new validated jobs.
10. A challenger model is trained and evaluated against the current champion.
11. Business safeguards and slice performance are reviewed.
12. The challenger is rejected or approved.
13. Approved models receive versioned artifacts.
14. Deployment is controlled, reversible, and requires human approval.
15. Drift, calibration, overrides, acceptance, and margin are monitored.

## Retraining Triggers

Use the more conservative trigger until real data volume is known:

- Monthly only when enough new validated jobs exist, or
- after 25-50 new validated completed jobs, whichever is later, or
- earlier only for material drift confirmed by review.

Small batches should update reports and comparable-job retrieval first. They should not automatically replace a Production model.

## Promotion Gate

A challenger can be promoted only after:

- it beats deterministic rules and simple historical baselines on the agreed metrics
- underpricing and severe-underpricing rates do not worsen
- gross-margin performance is acceptable
- service/geography/load/material slices are reviewed
- low-confidence and risky jobs remain manager-reviewed
- rollback to the prior champion is tested
- Tristan explicitly approves promotion

## Safeguards Outside The Model

- Minimum profitable price
- Travel-cost floor
- Disposal-cost floor
- Labor-cost floor
- Heavy-material restrictions
- Special-disposal requirements
- Multi-load review
- Demolition review
- Low-confidence review
- Maximum allowed model change
- Margin protection
- Customer-safe estimate status
- Manager override
- Emergency model disable
- Rollback to deterministic pricing

## Rollback

Keep the deterministic pricing rules and the prior champion model available. A rollback may disable model recommendations entirely and return to rules-only pricing. Rollback decisions should preserve the triggering inputs, model version, snapshot ID, and redacted error category for later review.
